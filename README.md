# My Secret Bedroom
Demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RCJf4FjnW0

## About the game
- My Secret Bedroom encircles the story of Ash/Ashley, a 19-year-old student in their bedroom and it seems that they have been struggling with mental health. This immersive, interactive game blends learning and recognising the signs of mental health distress such as Anxiety, Depression, Body Image Concerns, Eating Disorder, and indicators such as social withdrawal, low appetite, negative thoughts, etc.

## Game controls
-   Left joystick for movement
-	Right joystick for camera rotation (every 45 degrees)
-	Right joystick upwards to teleport around the place
-	Grab button for grabbing stuff
-	Trigger button to act as a “push”

# Game Features
## Game Interactables & intended interactions
### Calendar
- Player walks close to it
- Text appears asking the player to flip the calendar
- Calendar is highlighted
- Player flips (grabs) the calendar till August 2026
- Text moves on to the next
- Calendar is no longer highlighted

### Diary
- Player grabs the diary
- Diary opens
- Player clicks on one of the 3 entries to the diary
- Entry appears on the diary

### Clothes pile / Laundry
- Player goes near the clothes pile
- Text appears asking what they would do
- Player chooses Clean up room
- Clothes pile and basket is highlighted
- Player grabs the pile and drops it into the basket
- Basket becomes full visually and piles disappear
- Text shows up until the end
- Player chooses Lending a Ear
- Texts show up until the end

### Scale
- Player goes near the scale and looks at it
- Text appears asking a question
- Player nods/shakes head to answer
- Texts show up until the end

### Trash bin
- Player goes near the trash bin and looks at it
- Text appears asking a question
- Player nods/shakes head to answer
- Texts show up until the end

### Paper Assignments
- Player goes near the paper assignments and looks at it
- Text appears asking a question
- Player nods/shakes head to answer
- Texts show up until the end

### Mirror
- Player goes near the cloth and looks at it
- Text appears
- Cloth is highlighted
- Player removes the cloth
- Cloth is removed and mirror is shown

### Mobile Phone
- Player goes near the mobile phone and looks at it
- Text appears
- Player looks at phone and sees a voice message notification and plays it
- Voice recording plays

## Gameplay features
- Standing/Seated mode
- Nodding/Shaking detection mechanic for some of the interactables
- Highlighting as a guide for some interactable tasks

# Bugs/Limitations
- Shaking may not be registered as the reference point starts the very moment the player looks at the interactable edge.
- The poke interaction for the play button on the voicemail does not work for some reason. The play button itself is fine.
